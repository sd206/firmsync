## 4. Who FirmSync is for

**Headline:** A good fit if…

**Body (list):**
- You run a consulting firm with fewer than 50 people
- You bill clients by the hour and pay consultants or contractors at a cost rate
- Month-end invoicing takes days, or lives in spreadsheets and two or three apps
- You want to know which clients and projects actually make money

**Not the right fit if…**
- You need resource scheduling, capacity planning, a CRM or proposals: a full PSA suite fits better
- You're looking for accounting software: FirmSync works alongside your accountant, not instead of them

## 5. Your data

**Headline:** Your firm's data stays your firm's.

**Body:** Each firm's data is kept apart in the database, and consultants only see their own time. Card payments are handled by Stripe; FirmSync never sees your card details. If you leave, your firm stays readable for 120 days so you can download what you need.
