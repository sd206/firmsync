## 3. How it works

**Headline:** Hours in. Invoices out. Money tracked.

**Steps (numbered, left to right):**
1. **Consultants submit their week.** Weekly timesheets, submitted for approval.
2. **You approve.** Admins and managers approve or reject with a comment. Rates are frozen on approval.
3. **Build the invoice from approved hours.** One line per consultant, project and rate, with their task notes. Add fees, a discount and tax.
4. **Issue it.** The next invoice number, a PDF, and the hours locked so they're never billed twice.
5. **Record the payment.** The invoice moves to partly paid or paid on its own.

**Button:** See every feature → `/features`
